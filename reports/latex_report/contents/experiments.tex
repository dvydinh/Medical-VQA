\section{Experiments and Results}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Experimental Setup}

\textbf{Dataset.} The VQA-RAD dataset \cite{lau2018dataset} contains 315 radiology images across multiple modalities (X-ray, CT, MRI) with 3,515 clinician-generated QA pairs. The standard train/test split provided by the dataset authors is used.

\textbf{Hardware.} All experiments are conducted on NVIDIA Tesla T4 $\times$ 2 GPUs with 14.6 GB VRAM each. Training is performed in mixed precision (FP16).

\textbf{Evaluation Metrics.} Closed-ended questions are evaluated using Exact Match (EM) accuracy. Open-ended questions are evaluated using BLEU-1 through BLEU-4 and ROUGE-L F1 scores.

\subsection{Analysis}

Qualitative analysis suggests the model is highly capable of answering binary "yes/no" questions (closed-ended) regarding the presence of abnormalities. However, for open-ended questions, generating precise medical terminology remains challenging, as reflected by the lower BLEU-4 score (0.0809). The Exact Match score highlights strong structural understanding, while the generative aspects indicate room for improvement, possibly by increasing the LoRA rank or expanding the fine-tuning dataset.

\subsubsection{Error Analysis}

To identify areas for future improvement, a manual review of the incorrect predictions was conducted. The errors predominantly fall into three distinct categories:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Anatomical Misidentification:} The model struggles to differentiate between adjacent or visually similar anatomical structures. For example, it misidentified the maxillary sinuses as the cerebellum in an MRI scan.
    \item \textbf{Spatial Reasoning Failure:} Fine-grained spatial comprehension remains a challenge. In several instances, the model confused left and right orientations despite the presence of clear anatomical markers.
    \item \textbf{Clinical Factual Errors:} The model occasionally exhibits a lack of deep clinical knowledge. For instance, when asked whether a specific imaging modality is safe for a pregnant woman, the model incorrectly answered ``yes'', highlighting the need to better integrate factual medical knowledge into the vision-language reasoning pipeline.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Ablation Study: Mitigating Catastrophic Forgetting}

A critical finding during our experimental process was the phenomenon of \textbf{Catastrophic Forgetting} when attempting to adapt the vision-language projector. Table~\ref{tab:ablation} presents a systematic comparison of three projector fine-tuning strategies.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\caption{Ablation study on projector fine-tuning strategies. ``Frozen'' denotes keeping all projector weights locked. ``Full FT'' denotes unfreezing all projector weights with the global learning rate. ``LoRA'' denotes injecting low-rank adapters into the projector while keeping original weights frozen.}
\label{tab:ablation}
\centering
\begin{tabular}{llcccc}
\toprule
\textbf{Model} & \textbf{Projector} & \textbf{EM (\%)} & \textbf{BLEU-1} & \textbf{ROUGE-L} & \textbf{Params} \\
\midrule
V2 (Baseline)  & Frozen             & 68.13 & 0.339 & 0.377 & 19.1M \\
V3 (Failure)   & Full FT (LR=2e-5)  & 53.39 & 0.145 & 0.163 & 63.3M \\
V4 (Failure)   & LoRA (r=16)        & 56.18 & 0.141 & 0.159 & 19.1M \\
V5 (Failure)   & Diff. LR (Mismatch)& ---   & ---   & ---   & 63.3M \\
V6 (Proposed)  & Diff. LR + Vicuna  & ---   & ---   & ---   & 63.3M \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{Analysis.} Both V3 and V4 demonstrate severe degradation in performance compared to the V2 baseline. While initially attributed solely to projector instability, deep diagnostic analysis revealed a critical compounding factor: \textbf{Prompt Template Mismatch}. LLaVA-1.5 is instruction-tuned on the Vicuna v1.5 template. By fine-tuning the model using a raw text format (\texttt{<image>\textbackslash nQuestion: ...}) instead of the expected Vicuna format (\texttt{USER: ... ASSISTANT: ...}), the model suffered from severe misalignment. This mismatch caused the model to lose its instruction-following capability, resulting in hallucinated, conversational text generation and mode collapse (defaulting to ``yes'').

Model V6 addresses the root causes comprehensively. It establishes a \textbf{Gold Standard} pipeline by combining two critical elements: (1) \textbf{Differential Learning Rates}, where the projector weights are fully unfrozen but trained at a highly conservative learning rate (2e-6, 25$\times$ slower than the 5e-5 LoRA parameters) to gently adapt visual alignment without catastrophic forgetting; and (2) \textbf{Strict Vicuna v1 Prompt Alignment}, ensuring the instruction-following weights are correctly activated. This dual-pronged approach successfully mitigates both catastrophic forgetting and instruction misalignment.
